# How to Change your Password





At the top right of the screen, click the **User icon** and select **Profile**:

<img src="./images/image-20250502094530123.png" alt="image-20250502094530123" style="zoom:80%;" />



Enter a new **password** and re-type:

<img src="./images/image-20250502094723987.png" alt="image-20250502094723987" style="zoom:80%;" />

Click **Update**
