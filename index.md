## Welcome to the Krestfield Knowledge Base

Here you can find information on products, integration guides, white papers, tips and tricks

Follow the links below for information on specific products or use the search option above

### Products

- [Certdog](./certdog/certdog.html)

  - Our certificate manager, interfacing to Microsoft ADCS and other CAs providing a full RESTful API and intuitive UI to make issuance, renewal and management of certificates as easy as it should be

- [EzSign](./ezsign/ezsign.html)

  - A digital signature generation and verification server, interfacing to all popular HSMs

- [Signature Toolkit](./signature%20toolkit/signaturetoolkit_user_guide.html)

  - Create, verify and analyse PKCS#7/CMS based signatures. Supports PQC algorithms.

- [CRL OCSP Monitor](./ocsp-monitor/ocsp-monitor.html)

  - Monitor all your own and any external CRLs and OCSP responders that you may depend on. Get early notifications of potential issues

- [OCSP Responder](./certdog/ocsp.html)

  - Allows for rapid deployment of a responder in Windows environments with no need for IIS. Interfaces directly with Microsoft ADCS
